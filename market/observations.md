# Market Observations

Structured data: [`./competitor-matrix.yaml`](./competitor-matrix.yaml). Deep-dives: [`./competitors/`](./competitors).

## The gap

No tool today is **simple, AI-native, and sovereign** at once. That three-way gap is Otopic's market ([`../strategy/positioning.md`](../strategy/positioning.md)).

- **Messengers** (Slack, Teams) are where remote teams talk, but knowledge ends up in threads; their AI recaps the threads rather than giving work a durable home. Otopic doesn't replace them ([`../decisions/0004-own-line-not-a-replacement.md`](../decisions/0004-own-line-not-a-replacement.md)).
- **All-in-one workspaces** (Notion) are flexible but AI-partial, multi-tenant only, and store work as rich-text blobs AI parses poorly.
- **Work management** (Asana, ClickUp) is powerful but complex, AI-bolted-on, and closed to regulated industries.
- **Dev trackers** (Jira) exclude non-technical clients entirely.
- **Self-hosted** options deliver sovereignty but are clunky: no AI, dated UX, ops burden on the customer.

## Structural observations

1. **Regulated industries are an underserved premium segment.** Legal, healthcare, biotech, and finance are locked out of the modern tools entirely — not price-sensitive, compliance-blocked. BYOC unlocks them.
2. **Incumbents can't easily go text-first.** Their data models (nested boards, block trees, visual state) are their moat _against humans switching_ but their weakness _for AI_. Retrofitting AI-readable storage means rebuilding the core.
3. **Client collaboration is nobody's first-class feature.** Every incumbent treats external guests as an afterthought (seat pricing, permission hacks). For client-facing SME teams — agencies, consultancies, professional services — it's the whole job.
4. **Remote/hybrid work is permanent**, and the incumbents' answer (more dashboards, more meetings-in-app) doesn't solve async context loss ([`../product/use-cases/remote-work.md`](../product/use-cases/remote-work.md)).
5. **"Ask your workspace" is table stakes by 2026.** Microsoft 365 Copilot, Slack AI, Atlassian Rovo and Notion AI all answer questions across a workspace. AI Q&A alone doesn't differentiate; what it runs over (text-first topics → cited answers), where it runs (the customer's cloud) and who can ask (clients, on shared topics) do ([`../product/agent.md`](../product/agent.md)).

## Watch list

- Notion AI's evolution — closest to "AI-native workspace" messaging.
- Incumbent workspace AI (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Slack AI, Atlassian Rovo) — especially any move into client-facing access or customer-cloud deployment.
- Any incumbent announcing BYOC / customer-cloud deployment.
- New entrants combining two of the three attributes.
