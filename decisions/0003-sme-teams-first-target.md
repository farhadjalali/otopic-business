# 0003 — SME teams, not freelancers, are the first target

- **Date:** 2026-08-11
- **Status:** accepted (supersedes the freelancer-first wedge in [`../archive/otopic-business-plan-v1-2026-08-11.md`](../archive/otopic-business-plan-v1-2026-08-11.md))

## Context

The v1 plan positioned freelancers & small teams (2–15) as the wedge, with a viral "one freelancer brings Otopic to 3–5 client engagements" motion. Re-deriving targets from the product and channel exposed three problems:

1. **Channel mismatch** — Phase 1 GTM is Otopic inside Sapilon engagements. Sapilon's clients are companies; the plan's own distribution produces SME references, not freelancer users. Freelancer-first required a second acquisition motion built from zero.
2. **Weak monetization** — freelancers churn and under-pay; a freelancer-heavy Free tier turns the AI quota into unrecovered marketing burn. SME teams buy seats in batches and can price the ROI of AI-drafted status updates.
3. **No bridge to premium** — freelancers don't lead anywhere. Client-facing services SMEs (agencies, consultancies, and especially professional services like law/accounting) are the same buyer as the regulated Sovereign segment, earlier and smaller — one continuous market instead of two disconnected ones.

## Decision

- **Primary target: SME teams (~5–50 people)**, sharpest sub-segment client-facing services.
- **Premium: regulated & enterprise** via Sovereign (unchanged).
- **Freelancers: demoted to a later self-serve layer** — served by the Free tier, no launch marketing, no roadmap pull.
- Expansion motion becomes **client-side exposure**: every SME project invites client stakeholders; clients bring Otopic into their own orgs.

## Consequences

- GTM, pricing copy, personas, and use-cases reframed around team leads, not solo operators.
- Slightly more sales touch than a viral freelancer motion; mitigated by the Sapilon engagement pipeline and the client-exposure loop.
- Free tier's role shifts from "freelancer home" to "team trial + client-side seats + future freelancer layer."
- Watch: if self-serve freelancer signups organically outpace SME conversion in Phase 2, revisit.
