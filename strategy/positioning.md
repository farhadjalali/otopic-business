# Positioning

## One-liner

**Otopic is the project brain that lives in your cloud — AI-native collaboration for teams and their clients, with sovereignty by design.**

## The wedge

No tool today is simple, AI-native, _and_ sovereign. Existing tools force a trade-off between the three; Otopic delivers all of them. Any single attribute is replicable — **the combination is the moat.**

## Our own line

Otopic doesn't replace the messenger or the tracker; it replaces the **patchwork** around them — the board, the thread, the doc and the WhatsApp group where a remote team's context gets lost. For the SME motion the line is **simplicity and AI-native design**; sovereignty is the third leg for the regulated segment. ([`../decisions/0004-own-line-not-a-replacement.md`](../decisions/0004-own-line-not-a-replacement.md))

> _Keep chatting anywhere. Otopic is where your work and its decisions live — and you can ask it anything._

Users meet the AI through one agent they talk to or chat with ([`../product/agent.md`](../product/agent.md)).

## What Otopic is

- **A project brain** — every Topic remembers its context, decisions, and history; AI drafts updates, surfaces stalls, and answers "where are we on this?"
- **Sovereign** — deployable to the client's own AWS organization. Data never leaves their boundary; compliance becomes architectural, not contractual.
- **Simple by default** — mobile-first, three-tap actions, no setup overhead.
- **Minimal and text-first** — work is stored as structured, human-readable text that AI parses accurately, not opaque rich-text blobs or visual state.

## What Otopic is not

- A task manager with a chatbot bolted on
- A kanban/board tool optimized for human scanning but opaque to AI
- A multi-tenant SaaS that regulated industries can't touch
- A replacement for Slack, Teams or Jira — it has its own line (above)

## The problem we position against

- **Work is fragmented** across calendars, chats, docs, and task boards — context evaporates between them.
- **AI is bolted on**, not embedded. Most tools added a chatbot; none made AI the way work gets organized.
- **SME teams can't staff the overhead.** A 5–50 person team has real coordination and client-communication load but no capacity for a dedicated PM writing status updates.
- **Sovereignty is incompatible with modern tools.** Legal, healthcare, finance, and enterprise can't use Notion/Asana/ClickUp because the data sits in someone else's cloud.
- **Modern tools are opaque to AI.** Nested boards and rich-text blobs are optimized for human scanning, not machine parsing.
