# Vision & Mission

## Vision

Otopic is not a task manager. It is **a memory of work, a decision ledger, and a knowledge graph for execution** — running on infrastructure the customer owns.

> *Convert your business actions into data. Let AI make sense of it. Keep the data yours.*

## Mission

- **Why** — Work is fragmented across calendars, chats, docs, and boards; AI is bolted on instead of embedded; and regulated teams are locked out of modern tools because their data can't live in someone else's cloud.
- **What** — An AI-native project brain where the **Topic** is the durable unit of work: notes, chat, tasks, decisions, and AI context in one place.
- **How** — Simple by default, text-first by design, deployable to the customer's own cloud (BYOC).

## Core philosophy

Non-negotiable principles:

1. **The Topic is the unit of work.** Everything — notes, chat, tasks, decisions, AI context — lives in a durable container that remembers.
2. **AI is the way work gets organized, not a chatbot on the side.**
3. **Sovereignty is architectural, not contractual.** Data never leaves the customer's boundary.
4. **Text-first storage.** Structured, human-readable text is the source of truth; visual presentation is a read layer. (See [`../decisions/0001-text-first-data-model.md`](../decisions/0001-text-first-data-model.md).)
5. **Simple by default.** Mobile-first, three-tap actions, no setup overhead — for freelancers as much as enterprise.

## Long-term vision

Every Topic remembers its own context, decisions, and history. AI uses that memory to draft updates, surface what's stalled, and answer "where are we on this?" — no one writes a status report again.
