# Target Customers

Two segments, two motions — a bottom-up wedge and a top-down premium.

## Wedge — Freelancers & small teams (2–15 people)

**Pain:** the client-collaboration gap. Updates scattered over email, WhatsApp, and shared docs; no shared source of truth per engagement.

**What Otopic gives them:** a shared workspace where every project has a note, a chat, and an AI that keeps clients informed — with zero setup overhead.

**Viral motion:** one freelancer brings Otopic into 3–5 client engagements. Each engagement exposes new users on the client side.

**Motion:** self-serve, hosted SaaS (Free → Pro). Upgrade trigger is the AI quota (see [`../pricing/current-model.md`](../pricing/current-model.md)).

## Premium — Regulated & enterprise teams

**Who:** legal, healthcare, biotech, financial services, and enterprises with strict data policies.

**Pain:** currently locked out of modern AI collaboration tools entirely — the data cannot sit in a third-party multi-tenant cloud.

**What Otopic gives them:** BYOC deployment into their own AWS organization. Compliance becomes architectural. Distributed enterprises can deploy per-region for multi-jurisdiction data residency while sharing one project brain.

**Motion:** top-down, sold through Sapilon engagements as the Sovereign tier (see [`./sapilon-synergy.md`](./sapilon-synergy.md)).
