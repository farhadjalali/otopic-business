# Go-to-Market — Three Phases

Anchored to today's date, 2026-08-11, the phases below run from the plan's start (month 0 ≈ mid-2026).

## Phase 1 (months 0–9, ~mid-2026 → early 2027): Internal tool

Otopic is used inside every Sapilon engagement. **No public marketing.**

- Goal: real usage data and **5–10 reference clients**, funded by services revenue.
- Every engagement ships a customized, sovereign Otopic instance as part of the deliverable.

## Phase 2 (months 9–24, ~2027 → mid-2028): Standalone product

Public launch with **Free + Standard + Teams** tiers.

- **Sovereign** tier sold through Sapilon as a packaged offering.
- Content marketing on AI + project execution drives both demand funnels (Otopic and Sapilon).

## Phase 3 (year 2+, ~mid-2028): Decision point

Otopic either **spins out** as an independent product company (separate funding and team) or remains **Sapilon's flagship product**.

The architecture and brand are deliberately kept spin-out-ready so both options stay open. Revisit as a `decisions/` entry when the time comes.

## Motions by segment

| Segment | Motion | Entry point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SME teams (~5–50, client-facing services first) | Seeded via Sapilon engagements + client-side exposure (clients on a project bring Otopic into their own orgs) | Free team trial → trial-credit upgrade to Teams |
| Regulated / enterprise | Top-down via Sapilon engagements | Enterprise tier, packaged with services |
| Freelancers (later, Phase 2+) | Pure self-serve, no marketing spend | Free tier |
