# Sapilon Synergy

Otopic and Sapilon (AI software services) are **one offer with two revenue streams**: services (Sapilon) + recurring product (Otopic).

## Three shared layers

1. **Technical core** — every Sapilon AI engineering capability serves Otopic and vice versa.
2. **Go-to-market** — one thought-leadership engine (AI + project execution content) drives both demand funnels.
3. **Reference clients** — Sapilon engagements ship a customized, sovereign Otopic instance as part of the deliverable. The client becomes both a Sapilon revenue line *and* an Otopic logo.

## Pricing alignment

The Sovereign tier aligns with Sapilon's pay-as-you-go model: **AI credits + AWS pass-through + subscription + labor when needed.** See [`../pricing/current-model.md`](../pricing/current-model.md).

## Tension to manage

Sapilon emergencies can steal Otopic development time. Mitigation (see [`./risks.md`](./risks.md)): ring-fenced engineering hours per week protected from reallocation, plus a revenue-percentage rule that funds Otopic R&D.

## End state

By year 2+ the relationship resolves at the GTM decision point ([`./go-to-market.md`](./go-to-market.md)): spin-out vs. flagship product.
