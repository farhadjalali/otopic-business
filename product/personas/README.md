# Personas

One file per persona: who they are, their pain, how Otopic fits, what makes them convert.

Starting candidates (from [`../../strategy/target-customers.md`](../../strategy/target-customers.md)):

- The agency / consultancy team lead (~5–50 people) coordinating projects, contractors, and clients
- The account manager who owns client communication and drowns in status updates
- The compliance-bound partner (legal / accounting / healthcare) locked out of SaaS tools
- The client-side stakeholder who just wants to know "where are we on this?"
- (Later, Phase 2+) The freelancer running multiple client engagements solo
