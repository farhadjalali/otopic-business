# Use-case — Client Collaboration (the wedge)

**Who:** freelancers and small teams (2–15 people) working with external clients.

## The pain

The client-collaboration gap: updates scattered over email, WhatsApp, and shared docs. The client never knows where things stand; the freelancer burns hours on status communication.

## The Otopic answer

A shared workspace where every project (Topic) has:

- a **note** — the durable source of truth,
- a **chat** — conversation attached to the work, not lost in a messenger,
- an **AI** — that keeps clients informed automatically.

Client-facing views let external stakeholders **self-serve** instead of pinging: "where are we on this?" is answered by the Topic, not by a person.

## Why it spreads

One freelancer brings Otopic into 3–5 client engagements. Each engagement onboards people on the client side — the viral motion behind the Free tier (see [`../../strategy/target-customers.md`](../../strategy/target-customers.md)).
