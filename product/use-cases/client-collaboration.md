# Use-case — Client Collaboration (the wedge)

**Who:** client-facing SME teams (~5–50 people) — agencies, consultancies, studios, professional services — coordinating work across their own team *and* external clients.

## The pain

The client-collaboration gap, multiplied by team size: updates scattered over email, WhatsApp, and shared docs; the account lead spends hours a week on status communication; the client never knows where things stand; internally, nobody has capacity to play full-time PM.

## The Otopic answer

A shared workspace where every engagement (Topic) has:

- a **note** — the durable source of truth,
- a **chat** — conversation attached to the work, not lost in a messenger,
- an **AI** — that drafts updates, surfaces stalls, and keeps clients informed automatically.

Client-facing views let external stakeholders **self-serve** instead of pinging: "where are we on this?" is answered by the Topic, not by a person. Client-branded views (Pro) make the workspace feel like the firm's own.

## Why it spreads

Every project invites client-side stakeholders into the Topic — on the Free tier, at no cost to anyone. Those clients experience Otopic as *the way this firm works*, then bring it into their own organizations. Expansion travels through the work itself, team to team (see [`../../strategy/target-customers.md`](../../strategy/target-customers.md)).
